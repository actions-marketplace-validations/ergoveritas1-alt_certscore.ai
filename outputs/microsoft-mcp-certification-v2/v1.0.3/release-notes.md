CertScore Web Privacy Scanner offers free, usage-limited scanning of public websites and evidence-backed review of cookies, trackers, CMPs and consent controls, privacy-policy and disclosure signals, observable form and third-party embed activity, and HTTPS/TLS.

Version 1.0.3 updates the certification package's product naming, website link, matching icon artwork, onboarding links, requirements, usage-limit disclosures, and authentication instructions. The four-tool workflow and Microsoft endpoint are unchanged.

Results support human review and are not legal advice, certification, or a compliance determination.
