CertScore.ai Website Privacy Scanner MCP offers free, usage-limited scanning of public websites and evidence-backed review of cookies, trackers, CMPs and consent controls, privacy-policy and disclosure signals, observable form and third-party embed activity, and HTTPS/TLS. Version 1.0.2 adds `certscore_get_report_evidence_page` so reviewers can page through the retained report projection alongside the existing scan, status, and bundle tools. Eligible scans may include bounded consent-choice observations; unavailable or unconfirmed evidence remains an explicit coverage limitation. Results are review aids, not legal advice, certification, or a compliance determination.
