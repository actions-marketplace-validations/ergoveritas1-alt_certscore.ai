## LinkedIn

What does your website ask visitors to share?

An email address. A date of birth. An open-ended enquiry. The details matter—and they are easier to review when everyone can see the same evidence.

CertScore.ai’s forms capture brings observed forms, field details and available form screenshots into your website privacy report.

You can:
• Find forms on scanned public pages.
• Inspect labels, required fields and checkbox states.
• Open a masked form screenshot when capture succeeds.
• Use review cues alongside supporting privacy findings to ask better questions about data collection and privacy notices.

For developers, that means a precise page and field to investigate. For agencies, a clearer client conversation. For privacy reviewers, a concrete starting point.

Our release includes a real capture from an owned test page: four fields, one matching screenshot, and no privacy findings. Finding a form does not make it a problem.
