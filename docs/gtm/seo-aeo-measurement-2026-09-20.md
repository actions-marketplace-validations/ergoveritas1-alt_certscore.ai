# SEO and answer visibility: release baseline

Use existing Search Console and consent-aware analytics. Keep private property exports and numeric baselines in the owner's local audit artifacts rather than this public repository. No scheduled scans, services, or paid tools are added.

Priority pages: homepage, GDPR scanner, cookie consent scanner, privacy policy scanner, Reject guide, RTB guide, GPC guide, and analytics troubleshooting guide. Keep product intent distinct from explanatory guides.

Review weekly; assess the direction after 4–8 weeks of publication and recrawling. Export the same date windows, property, search type, and filters. Separate brand queries from non-brand GDPR checker/scanner, cookie consent testing, tracking before consent, Reject testing, and GPC clusters. Compare individual query/page pairs before interpreting aggregate position or CTR changes.

Use existing consent-aware product analytics to inspect landing-page/referral visits → scan starts → completed scans → signups where those events and attribution are available. Do not count a CTA click as a completed scan. Preserve the consent boundary and report unattributed traffic separately. Google generative AI impressions are visibility, not proof of referral traffic or a conversion. Missing AI referrers cannot be reconstructed reliably.

Check URL inspection for the five new canonical pages and the two consolidated guide targets. Confirm sitemap discovery and last crawl before interpreting missing performance. Do not try to index CSS, API artifacts, private pages, or deliberate redirects from the exclusion report.

Count independently verified relevant editorial references and distinct referring domains. A concentrated backlink source is not proof of spam. Avoid bulk directory submissions and manufactured endorsements.

Deployment cost estimate: no additional runtime service, scan invocation, model call, provisioned capacity, or retention policy. Four small static social images and text assets add approximately 0.3 MB before compression. Incremental storage and delivery at current traffic are expected below $0.01/month; existing build/deployment infrastructure is reused.
